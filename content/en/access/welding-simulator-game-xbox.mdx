## Tips for Getting Productive Fast Once It Launches

- **Learn one technique at a time.** Jumping between MMA, MIG, and TIG early scatters your practice. Pick one, run low-stakes jobs, then move on.
- **Cut generously, then trim.** Metal you removed cannot be put back, but a slightly oversized part can always be adjusted.
- **Reinvest in the workshop before cosmetics.** Career mode money spent on capability compounds; money spent on looks does not.
- **Watch your travel speed.** In all three techniques, the most common beginner error is moving too fast and getting cold, weak joins.
- **Keep the shop organized.** Tool location matters when you are repeating a job dozens of times.
- **Test controls early.** Spend ten minutes in a controller layout menu before you commit to a control scheme for a long session.

These are general simulation-game principles, not verified mechanics — the game is unreleased, so treat them as a sensible starting framework rather than a tested meta.
